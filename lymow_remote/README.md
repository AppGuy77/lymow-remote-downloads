# Lymow Remote

Drive your Lymow One mower from Home Assistant with a live camera: two joysticks (or one), blade speed, deck height
and the light, full screen, in the sidebar or on your phone.

- Connects to your mower **only while you use it** — nothing runs in the background.
- Uses port **8790**, so it installs next to the Lymow Toolkit add-on (which keeps 8787).
- Lymow allows **one app connection per account** at a time: while you drive with Lymow Remote, the official app or a
  Toolkit on the same account loses its connection until you close Lymow Remote.
